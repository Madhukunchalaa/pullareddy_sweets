About Us

A Journey of 77 Years

At a time when India was just forming an identity of its own, one man had the fortitude and foresight to establish a unique identity for Kurnool and the sweets that represented this new found freedom. In 1948, Sri G. Pullareddy Garu began this sweet journey with a simple dream - to preserve and share the authentic taste of traditional Indian sweets.

Sri G. Pullareddy Garu (Founder) inspired by the core values of Sri Kasi Reddy Garu, his uncle and mentor, G. Pullareddy established a legacy built on trust, quality, and unwavering commitment to tradition. His vision was simple yet profound - to create sweets that would become an integral part of every celebration and bring families together.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission: Born in the heart of Kurnool, G. Pulla Reddy Sweets stands for purity, tradition, and heartfelt hospitality. Our mission is to preserve the soul of Andhra’s sweet-making heritage — crafting each delicacy with devotion, honesty, and the same warmth our founder believed in. From our hometown kitchens to families across the world, we continue to share the taste of Kurnool’s love and legacy — spreading joy, one sweet at a time.

Our Vision: To uphold the legacy of authentic Indian sweets by staying true to time-honored traditions and using only the finest ingredients. We strive to consistently deliver purity, taste, and quality in every bite—ensuring our customers experience the same trust and delight that have defined G. Pulla Reddy Sweets for generations.

Core Values

A Sweet Tradition: Each recipe is a legacy, carefully preserved and perfected over time to bring you the same taste that has delighted generations. From selecting the finest ingredients to following traditional methods with precision, we honor our heritage while delivering timeless flavors you can trust.

Headed by Tradition, Driven by Purity

Our Sweet Legacy

Traditional

From a modest beginning in a small town to becoming a cherished household name, we have been crafting authentic Indian sweets with love and expertise for generations. Every sweet carries a legacy of heritage, flavor, and cherished memories shared across families.

Purity

Rooted in tradition and crafted with care, our sweets are prepared using the finest ghee from our own cattle. Each bite delivers unmatched freshness and the pure taste of bliss. We uphold time-honored methods passed down through generations, ensuring authenticity in every piece. From sourcing to preparation, every step reflects our unwavering commitment to purity and quality.

Social Responsibility

We believe in giving back as much as we delight taste buds. By supporting education through schools and colleges, we are committed to uplifting communities and nurturing brighter futures.
